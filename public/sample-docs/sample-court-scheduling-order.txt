                              CIVIL CASE MANAGEMENT AND SCHEDULING ORDER

HON. JESSE M. FURMAN, United States District Judge:

The Court, having conducted an initial pretrial conference pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 16 on
May 28, 2026, and having considered the parties' Joint Proposed Case Management Plan, hereby
ORDERS as follows:

1.  RESPONSIVE PLEADING. Defendant shall file its Answer or other responsive pleading no
    later than July 1, 2026. Any motion to dismiss shall be fully briefed on the same schedule
    unless leave of Court is obtained in advance.

2.  FACT DISCOVERY. All fact discovery, including depositions, interrogatories, requests for
    production, and requests for admission, shall be completed no later than August 30, 2026.
    The parties shall meet and confer regarding a document preservation protocol within
    fourteen (14) days of this Order.

3.  EXPERT DISCLOSURES. Pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(a)(2), initial expert witness
    disclosures, including reports required under Rule 26(a)(2)(B), shall be served no later
    than September 29, 2026. Rebuttal expert disclosures shall be served within thirty (30)
    days thereafter. Expert depositions shall be completed within forty-five (45) days of
    initial expert disclosures.

4.  DISPOSITIVE MOTIONS. Any motion for summary judgment or other dispositive motion shall
    be filed no later than October 29, 2026. Opposition briefs shall be due twenty-one (21)
    days after service of the opening brief. Reply briefs shall be due fourteen (14) days
    after service of the opposition.

5.  FINAL PRETRIAL CONFERENCE. A final pretrial conference will be held on November 28, 2026
    at 10:00 a.m. in Courtroom 1105, Thurgood Marshall United States Courthouse,
    40 Foley Square, New York, NY 10007. The parties shall submit a Joint Pretrial Order,
    motions in limine, proposed voir dire questions, and proposed jury instructions no later
    than seven (7) days before the conference.

6.  TRIAL. Trial is scheduled to commence on December 28, 2026. The parties estimate trial
    will last approximately eight (8) days. This is a jury trial.

7.  AMENDMENTS. Any motion to amend the pleadings or join additional parties shall be filed
    no later than July 15, 2026.

8.  SETTLEMENT. The parties shall advise the Court by letter no later than November 1, 2026
    whether they consent to proceed before a Magistrate Judge for all purposes, or whether
    they request a settlement conference.

STATUTE OF LIMITATIONS NOTICE: Plaintiff's counsel is advised that any related state law
claims under N.Y. C.P.L.R. § 214 must be filed within 3 years of the accrual date of
January 15, 2024, i.e., no later than January 15, 2027. Failure to timely assert any such
claims may result in their permanent waiver.

The deadlines set forth in this Order shall not be modified except upon a showing of good
cause. Requests for extensions must be made by letter-motion filed on ECF at least five (5)
business days before the applicable deadline.

SO ORDERED.

Dated: June 1, 2026
       New York, New York
