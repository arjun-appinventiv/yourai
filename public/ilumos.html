How iLumos works

1
You provide the inputsThe patent claims (the elements to prove) and the accused product's technical documents (the evidence pool). Optionally, a chart you've already started.
2
iLumos drafts each row, groundedIt reads your documents, finds the passage that supports each claim element, writes the analysis, and cites the exact source. It never invents evidence.
3
You review and approveAccept, edit, or reject every suggestion. Weak or unsupported elements are confidence-flagged, not hidden. The legal judgment stays yours.
4
Export, litigation-readySend the finished element-by-element chart out as PDF or Word, with citations intact.

iLumos accepts

Patent claims — paste a patent number or the claim text
Product technical docs — text-based PDF / DOCX: spec sheets, datasheets, manuals
A partial chart — CSV / XLSX or pasted table you've started
Public product URLs — a product page to pull evidence from

It won't take / won't do

Image-only scans — no readable text; flagged for OCR, never skipped
The infringement verdict — that's your legal call, not the tool's
Legal or validity opinions — out of scope
Unverifiable marketing fluff — it won't cite a claim it can't ground

iLumos validates at ingest and fails loudly, not silently. Missing is never fabricated.

Missing a column
Renders an explicit "Not drafted yet" state with a one-click Draft this — grounded and confidence-flagged, never blank-filled.
Blank cells / bare claim list
Treated as work to do: retrieves candidate evidence and drafts reasoning per element, each confidence-badged.
Wrong product docs
"These reference 'X', not your product — right files?" It refuses to cite them until you confirm.
Unreadable / conflicting evidence
Surfaced, never guessed. Conflicts show both sources; low-confidence items route back to you or a senior reviewer.
No evidence at all
Says "not covered" and asks for a doc or URL. It never invents a citation to fill the gap.
